Chess – Account Deletion

This page explains how users of Chess can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  • Open the Chess app
  • Go to Settings → Account
  • Tap on Delete Account

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

📧 help.auraluxedev@gmail.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information
  • Game progress and stats
  • Chat messages

Data Retention Policy

  • All user data is permanently deleted within 7 days
  • No personal data is retained after deletion
