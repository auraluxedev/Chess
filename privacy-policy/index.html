Privacy Policy for Chess

Last Updated: April 2026

Welcome to Chess, developed and published by Auraluxe. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while playing our game. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By downloading, accessing, or playing Chess, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless multiplayer experience and improve our services, we may collect the following types of information:

A. Automatically Collected Data

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: In-game actions, match history, ELO ratings, play time, and features utilized.
  • User ID & Authentication: We generate a unique User ID via Firebase Authentication to securely manage your profile, matchmaking, and game progress across devices.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) for serving personalized ads.

B. Data Provided by You

  • Profile Information: Player name, avatar selection, and linked email address (if authenticated via Google/Email).
  • Communication: In-game chat messages (which are temporarily processed to deliver real-time gameplay).

2. In-App Purchases, Memberships & Diamond Top-Ups

Chess offers premium features, memberships, and in-game currency ("Diamonds") through in-app purchases.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system. Auraluxe does not collect, process, or store your credit card or financial information.
  • Purchase History: We track your transaction history (e.g., successful diamond top-ups or active membership status) securely in our Firebase Database to ensure your virtual items are correctly credited to your account.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We use trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services operate under their own Privacy Policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to understand how players interact with our game, track crashes, and optimize performance.
  • Firebase Firestore & Auth: Used for secure player authentication, real-time matchmaking, and saving game data.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may use advertising IDs to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including:

  • To facilitate real-time multiplayer matchmaking and synchronize chess boards.
  • To maintain your account, ELO rating, Diamond balance, and membership status.
  • To serve relevant advertisements via AdMob to keep the base game free.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or cheating.

5. Data Retention and User Deletion Rights

We retain your data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the App's services. Under Google Play's Data Safety guidelines, you have the right to request the deletion of your data.

  • Account Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account, game history, and purchased Diamond records by contacting us at the email provided below. Upon verification, your data will be permanently erased from our Firebase servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
